About Manipulens

Built for Media Literacy

In an age of information warfare, Manipulens empowers people to recognize psychological manipulation before it shapes their beliefs.

🎯 The Mission

Manipulens was built to address a critical gap in media literacy tools. Most fact-checkers verify what is said — we analyze how it's said. Manipulation often works not through lies, but through emotional exploitation, urgency, fear, and tribal signaling.


Our AI model is trained to recognize 7 distinct psychological manipulation vectors across any form of written content — from political tweets to marketing copy.
