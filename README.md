Manipulens 🔍

See Through the Manipulation.
An AI-powered psychological manipulation detector for tweets, headlines, ads, and any digital content.

---

What is Manipulens?

Manipulens is a media literacy tool that analyzes how text is written — not just what it says. While fact-checkers verify claims, Manipulens detects the emotional and psychological tactics used to influence your beliefs: fear, urgency, rage bait, clickbait, tribal signaling, and more.

Built as a submission for the AI for BUILD-A-THON 2026, Manipulens addresses a critical gap in the fight against digital manipulation and misinformation.

---

Features

- 🧠 Psychological Analysis — Detects fear, urgency, anger, and emotional pressure patterns using advanced prompt engineering
- 📊 7-Axis Scoring — Quantifies manipulation across 7 dimensions with a unified Manipulation Meter score (0–100)
- ⚡ Real-time AI — Powered by Grok AI for instant analysis
- 🎯 Bias Detection — Identifies political, emotional, commercial, and ideological bias
- 📋 Analysis History — Keeps a log of your past analyses in-session
- 📥 Export Results — Download results as PDF or copy to clipboard

How It Works

1. Paste any text — tweet, headline, ad copy, article excerpt
2. Analyze — Grok AI scores the content across 7 psychological manipulation dimensions
3. Review — Results are displayed as a Manipulation Meter score, radar chart, and written AI insight

Manipulation Dimensions Scored

| Dimension | What It Detects |
|---|---|
| Fear | Fear-inducing language and threat framing |
| Anger | Rage-bait and outrage triggers |
| Urgency | False urgency and pressure tactics |
| Clickbait | Curiosity gaps and sensationalist framing |
| Bias | Political, ideological, or commercial slant |
| Toxicity | Hostile or divisive language |
| Neutrality | Absence of balanced, factual tone |

Project Background

Manipulens was created to promote media literacy in an era of information warfare. The goal is to empower everyday users — not just researchers — to recognize psychological manipulation before it shapes their beliefs and decisions.
